WHY THIS IS THE KEYSTONE CONTROL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A payer cannot bypass the gateway because Azure grants the payer nothing.
  Their token is valid at Entra - and worthless at the FHIR service.
  Only the APIM managed identity can touch data. Bypass is not "blocked",
  it is unrepresentable. This is why assertion 12 returns 403.
